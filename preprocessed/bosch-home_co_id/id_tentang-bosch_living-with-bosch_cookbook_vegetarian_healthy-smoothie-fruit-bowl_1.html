Healthy Smoothie Fruit Bowl

Guten Appetit!

Difficulty: Easy

Servings: 4

Preparation Time: 300 mins (includes freezing fruits)

Cooking Time: 20 mins

*Product used in recipe is for illustration purposes only. Please check locally for product availability.

Download Recipe

Ingredients

2 Tablespoons Honey (optional) 30g Pumpkin Seeds (Deshelled)
4 Tablespoons Plain Greek Yoghurt 50g Green Kiwi
1 Banana (Sliced) 100g Blueberries
1 Mango (Cubed) 200g Raspberries
10g Chia Seeds 200g Strawberries
30g Dried Cranberries

Methods

1/2

1. Remove the green cap and stem of the strawberries and cut them into halves.

2. Place the raspberries, strawberry halves and blueberries into a clean storage box. Freeze the berries for about 5 hours or until the berries turn solid.

Tip: To seal in freshness, use the ‘Vacuum’ function with the Storage Box of the VitaMaxx Vacuum Blender.

1/7
1/5

3. Peel the kiwi and mango, and cut them into cubes. Peel the banana, and cut into slices.

4. In a Vacuum Blender Jar, add the frozen berries, plain Greek yoghurt and honey. Blend using the ‘Auto’ function, until smooth. If needed, add about 100ml of water.

Tip: The Vacuum Blender will first start vacuuming, and proceed to blend.

1/6
1/5

5. Portion the smoothie into bowls, and top with the mango and kiwi cubes, banana slices, dried cranberries, deshelled pumpkin seeds and chia seeds.

6. Enjoy while fresh!
